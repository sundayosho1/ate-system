## A26-Q01 — OPEN; owner-accepted operational condition, not a fix

Admitted-queue attempt fairness is not universal admission fairness. Lexicographic-first admission
can starve a peer under continually advancing logical time, and repeated first-subscription failures
can prevent peer admission even at a fixed cutoff. Under audited conditions obligations and
continuation remain truthful. Drivers must not assume repeated advance() guarantees global progress;
inspect child Results and unresolved obligations, use an appropriate fixed logical-time cutoff for
finite draining where supported, and reconcile failed/uncertain work rather than blindly retrying
indefinitely. Restoration of the failing source or explicit peer admission through accepted
interfaces may be necessary. Preserve these restrictions in configuration/help and future phase
architecture reviews; future Prompt 27 attention policies must not assume universal admission
fairness. This proposal changes no help/configuration file; existing help is preserved, and future
work must carry the full condition forward. Remains OPEN, explicitly accepted as an operational
restriction, not resolved.
